# StatAttack

Pick a stat. Beat your mate. Take the card.

A turn-based stat battle card game for playing friends, in the spirit of the classic school-playground card game. Play the computer, challenge a friend with a link, or build your own deck.

- Play: https://bensoneastonltd.github.io/statattack/
- Game server: Supabase (deals the cards and checks every move)
